About Shaam Ki Chai Corner

Shaam Ki Chai Corner is inspired by the traditional Pakistani evening chai culture. We believe that a cup of hot chai and a plate of snacks can make an ordinary evening special. Our aim is to bring people together over good food and refreshing tea.

What Makes Us Special?

Freshly Prepared

We focus on serving fresh chai and snacks to our customers.

Traditional Taste

Our menu includes familiar desi favourites such as doodh patti, samosas, cake rusk and nimko.

A Welcoming Place

Our goal is to provide a comfortable place where friends and families can enjoy their evening together.
